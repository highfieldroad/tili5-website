Privacy

Updated 3 November 2026

Owner’s publishing tool

This policy covers TILI5 Shorts, the private tool used by the TILI5app channel owner. This information website has no account registration, advertising trackers or analytics installed by TILI5.

Google and YouTube data

With the owner’s consent, the tool receives OAuth access and refresh tokens, reads the authorized channel ID, uploads video files and metadata, and checks the resulting video IDs, recording dates, processing status and visibility. These permissions support publication and prevent uploads to the wrong channel.

Videos and their public metadata become available on YouTube when published. Authorization credentials remain in protected local configuration and GitHub Actions secrets. Publication records are stored in a private GitHub repository.

Processing and retention

GitHub hosts production runs and their private records. OpenAI helps research, write and review scripts and images; ElevenLabs generates narration from scripts. Google authorization tokens and retrieved private account data are not included in those AI requests. YouTube account data is not used to train AI models, sold, or used for advertising.

Video build artifacts are retained for 14 days, editorial artifacts for 30 days, and publication records until the owner removes them. Uploaded videos remain on YouTube until the owner removes them there. Hosting providers may retain technical access logs under their own policies.

Control and contact

The owner can stop scheduled runs, remove stored credentials and records, or revoke Google access. Revocation stops future authorized access; it does not delete videos already published.

Questions or deletion requests: cwoods.aus@gmail.com.

TILI5’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. YouTube use is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.
